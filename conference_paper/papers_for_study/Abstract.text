Security Operations Centers are struggling with the operational challenge of increasing alert volumes and lack of effective tools that help with their prioritization to avoid analyst exhaustion and slow response. Security Information and Event Management systems (SIEMs) and rule-based architectures manage to trigger events in security when anomalies arise, but usually fail to associate it with the current risk to an organization. We propose a framework that uses explainable artificial intelligence for automatic threat triage by associating machine learning classifiers with an estimated business impact model. This hybrid approach allows for the integration of probabilistic results of supervised learning models such as Random Forest and Extreme Gradient Boosting with multiple objectives related to asset criticality, operational reliance and probable monetary loss. This will finally translate into a final score, computed by a combination of the business impacts as well as classifier probabilities which increases the correctness of rankings while also decreasing MTTD (mean time to detect) and MTTResponding (mean time to response) on widely known intrusion detection sets. The developed design combines business impact with technical threat identification and provides a solution in a scalable architecture suitable for use in high-priority industrial contexts.
